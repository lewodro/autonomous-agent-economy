## AgentMind
Stable identity, bounded lessons, opponent notes, goals and research summaries now form a structured mind layer. Existing canonical profiles migrate into it, builtin strategies can consume safe summaries and exports omit hidden chain-of-thought.

## Private creation
Inside an owner-scoped experiment, a visitor can choose an approved sprite, name and builtin controller for a user agent. Its research and results remain private to that experiment and do not enter the public Arena or rankings.

## Commit sequence
The agent_mind_001–003 commits introduced structured memory, safe controller context and legacy research projection. private_agent_001–002 added creation, persistence and browser export coverage. The accompanying architecture commit documented public, private and external-agent boundaries.
